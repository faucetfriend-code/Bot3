# Getting Alerts from TradingView to Alertatron

The fastest and easiest way to get alerts from TradingView to Alertatron is by using webhooks.

## Steps:

1.  **Set up an alert in TradingView**: Create a new alert or edit an existing one within TradingView.
2.  **Enable Webhook URL**: In the TradingView alert dialog box, locate and tick the "Webhook URL" option.
3.  **Enter Alertatron Webhook Address**: Input your unique Alertatron Incoming Webhook address into the provided text field.

Once configured, every alert from TradingView will be sent to Alertatron. From Alertatron, the alerts can be forwarded to other services like Telegram or Discord.

## Additional Features:

*   **Chart Capturing**: Alertatron can capture charts with your alerts.
*   **Dynamic Alerts**: You can include the current price and other indicator values in your alert messages.

## Strategy Overview

The Liquidation Capture strategy fades post-liquidation cascades by detecting:
- 5-bar price drop >= `price_threshold`
- Volume spike >= `volume_mult` × 20-bar average
- RSI <= `rsi_threshold` (oversold confirmation)
- >= 4 consecutive down closes
- Lower wick ratio >= 1.5 (panic selling signature)
- Exit: TP = 3× risk (3:1 RRR), SL = 1% beyond 5-bar extreme

### Recommendations

1. **If daily data is too restrictive**: The strategy may work better on 4h or 1h timeframes
   where liquidation cascades are more frequent and detectable.
2. **Consider ATR-based thresholds**: Fixed % thresholds don't adapt to volatility regimes.
   Using ATR multiples would make the strategy more robust across different market conditions.
3. **Relax the consecutive down candle requirement**: The current 4-candle requirement
   is very strict. Consider reducing to 3 or making it configurable.
4. **Add volume profile confirmation**: Liquidation events often occur at key support/resistance
   levels. Adding volume profile or reference level confirmation could improve signal quality.
5. **Consider asymmetric parameters**: Long and short entries may need different thresholds
   since crypto markets have different dynamics for crashes vs. squeezes.

Reason: Prioritizes risk minimization in trending markets where grids can bleed from one-sided moves.
Why Full Flatten?: Per the Grid Trading Brief (implied in docs), grids assume no directional bias. Trending regimes invalidate this, so everything unwinds to avoid drawdowns. No partial logic exists currently—positions aren't classified as "with/against trend."

Trace: No bugs here; it's intentional for safety. Logs would show "Grid disabled by regime change" followed by "All orders cancelled" and "Position flattened."

1. Volume Profile & Order Flow
What: Analyze where the most trading volume occurred at specific price levels.
Implementation:

Identify Point of Control (POC): Price with highest volume = strong support/resistance
Value Area: 70% of day's volume range = fair value zone
Enter near value area extremes, target POC

Why it works: Institutional traders leave footprints in volume. High-volume nodes = areas where big money accumulated/distributed.

3. Previous Day High/Low (PDH/PDL)
What: Use yesterday's extremes as key levels.
Implementation:

Enter on breakout above PDH (bullish continuation)
Enter on breakdown below PDL (bearish continuation)
Or fade at PDH/PDL if signs of rejection appear

Why it works: Market has memory. Previous extremes are psychological levels where orders cluster.
4. VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)
What: Average price weighted by volume—shows "fair value."
Implementation:

Price above VWAP = bullish bias, buy dips to VWAP
Price below VWAP = bearish bias, sell rallies to VWAP
Strong moves away from VWAP often revert

Why it works: Institutional benchmark. Many algos and traders use VWAP for execution.
5. Smart Money Concepts (SMC) - Order Blocks
What: Identify the last bullish candle before a drop (bearish order block) or last bearish candle before rally (bullish order block).
Implementation:

Mark the order block zone
Enter when price returns to test that zone
Confirms institutional "unfilled orders" at that level

Why it works: Institutions leave limit orders that get filled on retests, creating reliable support/resistance.
6. Multi-Timeframe MA Confluence
What: Stack multiple moving averages (20/50/200) and trade bounces when aligned.
Implementation:

When 20 > 50 > 200 = strong uptrend
Buy when price pulls back to 20 MA
Stop below 50 MA
Exit if price closes below 50 MA

Why it works: MA stacking shows trend strength. Institutions respect major MAs (especially 200).
7. Gap Fill Strategy
What: Markets tend to "fill" overnight gaps (return to previous close).
Implementation:

If gap up >1%, short when price shows weakness, target gap fill
If gap down >1%, long when price shows strength, target gap fill
70%+ of gaps fill within 5 days

Why it works: Gaps create imbalances; markets seek equilibrium.
8. Time-Based Patterns
What: Certain times have predictable behavior.
Implementation:

9:30-10:00 AM: Avoid (choppy, institutional positioning)
10:00-11:30 AM: Best trend-following window
2:00-3:00 PM: Reversal window (position squaring)
3:00-4:00 PM: Trend continuation or reversal

Why it works: Institutional trading flows follow schedules.

I. MANDATORY RISK MANAGEMENT RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
A. Position Sizing - Isolated Margin Model
Core Principle: Allocate a fixed percentage of account as margin per trade. Maximum loss per trade is capped at the margin allocated, not calculated as percentage of total position value.
Position Sizing Framework:
Account Balance: $1,000
Margin Allocation Per Trade: $100-200 (10-20% of account)
Leverage: 10-20x (typical: 15x)
Position Value: Margin × Leverage

Example:
$100 margin × 15x leverage = $1,500 position value
Maximum loss if liquidated: $100 (10% of account)
Risk Tiers by Trade Quality:

Critical Rules:

Never allocate more than 20% of account as margin on a single trade
Maximum 3 concurrent positions (30% total margin allocation)
Each position is isolated - liquidation of one doesn't affect others
Leverage calculation: Position Value = Margin × Leverage
Account risk is NOT position value × stop %, it's: (Position Value × Stop %) ÷ Account Balance

C. Liquidation Risk Management
Understanding Liquidation:
Liquidation occurs when: Position Loss = 100% of Margin Allocated
At 15x leverage: Liquidation at 6.67% price movement against you
At 10x leverage: Liquidation at 10% price movement against you
At 20x leverage: Liquidation at 5% price movement against you

Formula: Liquidation Distance % = (1 ÷ Leverage) × 100
Liquidation Safety Requirements:

Minimum buffer: 1.5x your stop distance
Example: 4% stop requires 6% liquidation point minimum

4% × 1.5 = 6% minimum liquidation distance
15x leverage liquidates at 6.67% ✅ (safe)
20x leverage liquidates at 5% ❌ (too tight)



Liquidation Buffer Table:

RULE: If your stop distance × 1.5 > (1 ÷ leverage), reduce leverage or tighten stop.
D. Reward-to-Risk Ratio (RRR)
Minimum acceptable RRR: 2:1
Target RRR: 3:1 or higher
Rule: If a trade setup doesn't offer minimum 2:1 RRR, reject the trade.
Calculation:
Risk = Entry Price - Stop Price
Reward = Target Price - Entry Price
RRR = Reward ÷ Risk

Example:
Entry: $45,000
Stop: $43,650 (3% below) → Risk = $1,350
Target: $48,600 (8% above) → Reward = $3,600
RRR = $3,600 ÷ $1,350 = 2.67:1 ✅ (acceptable)
Target Setting Methods:

Next major resistance level (preferred)
Multiple of risk taken (2x, 3x the risk distance)
Fibonacci extension levels
Prior swing high/low

E. Time-Stops
Purpose: Exit trades that show no movement within predefined timeframe to free up capital.
Implementation:

15-minute chart trades: Exit if no 0.5% movement in 2 hours
Swing trades: Exit if no 1% movement in 4 hours
Prevents "dead money" syndrome
Frees capital for better opportunities

III. TRADE QUALIFICATION CRITERIA
A. Market State Identification
Determine current phase before strategy selection:
TREND Phase (Positive Feedback):

Directional price movement (clear higher highs/lower lows)
Expanding price bars
Rising volume on directional moves
Moving averages aligned and sloping
Strategy: Trend-following ONLY

RANGE Phase (Negative Feedback):

Sideways movement (horizontal consolidation)
Contracting price bars
Declining volume
Moving averages flat or intertwined
Strategy: Range trading OR wait for breakout

TRANSITION Phase (Highest Risk):

Conflicting signals across timeframes
Choppy, erratic price action
Volume inconsistent
Moving averages converging
Strategy: AVOID - wait for clarity

B. Entry Signal Requirements
A trade is ONLY valid when ALL conditions are met:
1. Clear Trend Identification:

Uptrend: Series of higher highs AND higher lows on daily/4H chart
Downtrend: Series of lower highs AND lower lows on daily/4H chart
Visual confirmation: Price action clearly directional, not choppy

2. Pattern Confirmation:

Pattern must show "warts and bruises" (imperfection reduces crowd participation)
REJECT "perfect" textbook patterns (these are crowd traps)
Pattern must break out decisively before entry
Breakout confirmation: Price closes beyond pattern boundary with volume

3. Volume Confirmation:

Price move MUST be confirmed by volume increase
High volume = conviction (institutional participation)
Low volume = suspect move, likely to fail, AVOID
Breakout volume should be 1.5-2x average volume

4. Multi-Timeframe Alignment:

All three timeframes (daily, 15-min, 5-min) must support trade direction
No conflicting signals between timeframes
If 15-min says buy but daily says sell → NO TRADE

5. Support/Resistance Validation:

Entry near support for longs (within 1-2%)
Entry near resistance for shorts (within 1-2%)
Clear space to profit target (no major obstacles in between)
Support/resistance must be tested previously (shows validity)

6. Favorable RRR:

Minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk
Profit target based on next major resistance/support
Clear, logical profit target (not arbitrary)

7. Stop-Loss in Sweet Spot:

Stop distance between 3-5% from entry
Stop at logical technical level
Liquidation buffer minimum 1.5x stop distance

8. No Forbidden Conditions Present (see Section V)

IV. STRATEGY RULESET
Primary Strategy: Trend-Following (75% of trades)
Philosophy: Trade in the direction of the primary trend, buying dips in uptrends and selling rallies in downtrends.
Long Entry Rules:

Price in established uptrend on daily chart (higher highs, higher lows)
Price pulls back to support/swing low on 15-min chart (2-4% pullback)
Volume confirms the pullback is exhaustion, not reversal (declining volume on pullback)
RSI shows oversold (<30) or rising from oversold (momentum turning)
Support level is clearly defined and previously tested
Entry: Above previous swing low with stop below support
Target: Next resistance level or 2-3x risk distance
Volume must increase on entry confirmation candle

Short Entry Rules:

Price in established downtrend on daily chart (lower highs, lower lows)
Price rallies to resistance/swing high on 15-min chart (2-4% rally)
Volume shows selling pressure resuming (high volume on red candles)
RSI shows overbought (>70) or falling from overbought
Resistance level is clearly defined and previously tested
Entry: Below previous swing high with stop above resistance
Target: Next support level or 2-3x risk distance
Volume must increase on entry confirmation candle

Trend-Following Confirmation Checklist:

 Daily trend clearly established (at least 3 swing points)
 Pullback/rally is against the trend (countertrend move)
 Pullback/rally is 2-4% deep (not too shallow, not too deep)
 Volume declining on countertrend move (exhaustion)
 Support/resistance level is clear
 RSI confirming (oversold for longs, overbought for shorts)
 Room to target with minimum 2:1 RRR
 Stop in 3-5% range

Secondary Strategy: Breakout Trading (20% of trades)
Philosophy: Enter when price decisively breaks out of consolidation pattern with strong volume, capitalizing on momentum.
Breakout Entry Rules:

Identify consolidation pattern (triangle, rectangle, flag, pennant)
Pattern must form over sufficient time (minimum 4 hours on 15-min chart)
Pattern boundary must be clearly defined
Breakout MUST occur with significantly higher volume (1.5-2x average)
Wait for candle close beyond support/resistance boundary
Enter on next candle open or on minor pullback to breakout level
Stop-loss: Inside the pattern boundary (below breakout point for longs)
Target: Measured move based on pattern height

Pattern Completion Requirements:

Triangle: At least 5 touches (3 on one side, 2 on other)
Rectangle: At least 2 touches of both support and resistance
Flag: Sharp pole followed by tight consolidation
Pattern must not be "too perfect" (some variance is healthy)

False Breakout Detection (AVOID THESE):

"Splash" of volume on breakout but price immediately rolls back into pattern
Perfect-looking breakout that everyone sees (crowd trap)
No follow-through after initial thrust (next candle reverses)
Breakout occurs on low volume (not confirmed)
Action: If detected, consider FADING the breakout instead (advanced)

Breakout Confirmation Checklist:

 Pattern clearly defined with multiple touches
 Pattern formed over 4+ hours
 Breakout volume is 1.5-2x average
 Breakout candle closes beyond pattern boundary
 No immediate rollback (next 2 candles follow through)
 Stop inside pattern boundary
 Measured move target achieves 2:1 RRR minimum
 Pattern shows imperfections (not textbook perfect)

Tertiary Strategy: Liquidation Event Capture (5% of trades)
Philosophy: Capitalize on extreme price movements caused by cascading liquidations, which create temporary oversold/overbought conditions ripe for reversal.
Liquidation Event Characteristics:

Sudden, violent price movement (>3% in under 15 minutes)
Extreme volume spike (3-5x average volume)
Rapid successive candle closes in same direction (5+ candles)
Price "waterfalls" through multiple support/resistance levels
Long wicks forming as price extends beyond rational levels

Long Entry Rules (After Downside Liquidation Cascade):

Extreme volume spike confirmed (3x+ the 20-period average)
Price drops 3%+ in under 15 minutes
RSI hits extreme (<15) indicating panic
Price reaches 3+ standard deviations from moving average (Bollinger Band extreme)
Reversal candle forms: Hammer, bullish engulfing, or long lower wick
Volume declining on the extreme candle (exhaustion)
Higher timeframe (daily/4H) shows major support nearby
WAIT for reversal candle to CLOSE before entering
Enter on next candle open or on retest of extreme low
Stop: 1-2% beyond the liquidation wick low
Target: Return to pre-liquidation level (minimum) or next resistance

Short Entry Rules (After Upside Liquidation Cascade):

Extreme volume spike confirmed (3x+ the 20-period average)
Price rises 3%+ in under 15 minutes
RSI hits extreme (>85) indicating euphoria
Price reaches 3+ standard deviations from moving average
Reversal candle forms: Shooting star, bearish engulfing, or long upper wick
Volume declining on the extreme candle (exhaustion)
Higher timeframe shows major resistance nearby
WAIT for reversal candle to CLOSE before entering
Enter on next candle open or on retest of extreme high
Stop: 1-2% beyond the liquidation wick high
Target: Return to pre-liquidation level (minimum) or next support

Liquidation Trade Position Sizing:

Use standard margin allocation ($100-150)
Leverage: 15-20x (acceptable due to higher probability)
Tighter stops acceptable (1.5-2.5%) due to clear invalidation point
Minimum RRR: 3:1 (liquidation trades should offer excellent reward)

Liquidation Event Safeguards:
DO NOT TRADE if:

Liquidation occurs during major news event (genuine price discovery, not just stops)
No clear reversal pattern forms within 3 candles of extreme
Higher timeframe shows no support/resistance nearby (middle of no man's land)
Second liquidation wave appears imminent (multiple liquidation clusters)
Market conditions are overall chaotic/unpredictable
You've already taken a liquidation trade in the last 4 hours

Maximum Liquidation Trades:

1 per trading session maximum
Must have 4+ hours between liquidation event trades
If stopped out, no second liquidation attempt same session

Volume below 0.7x the 20-period average
Thin trading conditions (wide bid-ask spreads)
Lack of market participation
Increased slippage risk
Action: Avoid trading, liquidity too low

3. News Events Pending

Major economic releases imminent (within 30 minutes)
High-impact news scheduled (check economic calendar)
Federal Reserve announcements
Major crypto protocol upgrades/launches
Unpredictable volatility likely
Action: Close positions before news or wait until after

Textbook-perfect setup visible to all traders
No "warts and bruises" on the pattern
Obvious setup that retail traders will pile into
Likely crowd trap set by smart money
Action: REJECT trade or wait for imperfect setup

5. Poor Risk-Reward

Cannot achieve minimum 2:1 RRR
Stop-loss too wide for account size (>6%)
Profit target too close to entry (<2x risk distance)
Obstacles (support/resistance) between entry and target
Action: REJECT trade, wait for better setup

6. Overtrading Conditions

3+ consecutive losses today
Daily loss limit reached (20% of account = $200)
Emotional state: frustrated, angry, desperate, euphoric
Taking trades out of boredom (no clear setup)
Trying to "make back" losses
Action: CEASE trading for 24 hours, mandatory break

7. Against Higher Timeframe Trend

Lower timeframe signal contradicts daily/4H trend
Attempting countertrend trade without exceptional confirmation
"Catching a falling knife" or "shorting a rocket"
Action: REJECT trade unless liquidation event setup

8. Stop-Loss Issues

Stop distance <2.5% (too tight for 15-min chart)
Stop distance >6% (too wide, excessive risk)
Stop not at logical technical level (arbitrary placement)
Liquidation buffer <1.5x stop distance (too close to liquidation)
Account loss if stopped >8%
Action: REJECT trade or adjust position sizing

9. Insufficient Margin Available

Current open positions already use 25%+ of account as margin
Taking new trade would exceed 30% total margin allocation
Not enough capital to properly size position
Action: Wait for current positions to close

10. Technical Issues

Platform connectivity problems
Unusually high latency (>500ms)
Order execution delays
Cannot set stop-loss orders properly
Action: Do not trade until technical issues resolved

B. Active Trade Management
Trailing Stop Protocol:
Initial Phase (0-1R profit):

Keep original stop-loss in place
No adjustments
Let trade develop

Breakeven Phase (1R profit reached):

Once trade moves 1:1 (risk distance in profit), move stop to breakeven
Calculation: If entry $45k, stop $43.65k (risk = $1.35k), move stop to $45k once price hits $46.35k
Purpose: Eliminate risk, guarantee no loss
Lock in: Zero loss minimum

Profit Lock Phase (2R profit reached):

Once trade moves 2:1, trail stop to lock in 1R profit
Calculation: If entry $45k, initial stop $43.65k, move stop to $46.35k once price hits $47.7k
Purpose: Guarantee profit even if reversal occurs
Lock in: 1R profit minimum

Trailing Stop Placement:

Use swing highs/lows as trailing stop reference points (technical levels)
Avoid moving stop too close to current price (needs breathing room)
Only move stop in favorable direction (never wider)
Trail by 50% of the move (e.g., if price moves $2k up, trail stop $1k up)

Partial Profit Taking (Optional Strategy):

At 2:1 RRR: Consider taking 50% position off
Benefits: Locks in guaranteed profit, reduces stress
At 3:1 RRR: Take another 25% off (75% total closed)
Let remaining 25% run to 4-5:1 or beyond
Move stop to breakeven on remaining position
Only use if RRR exceeds 3:1 initially

Position Monitoring Requirements:

Check position every 2-4 hours minimum
Monitor for trail stop adjustments
Watch for invalidation signals (see below)
Track funding rate costs (crypto perpetuals)
Ensure stop-loss order remains active

VII. ANTI-BIAS SAFEGUARDS
Cognitive Bias Prevention
1. Gambler's Fallacy Protection:
Definition: The mistaken belief that after a series of losses, a win is "due."
Manifestations:

"I've lost 3 in a row, next one HAS to be a winner"
Increasing position size after losses to "recover faster"
Taking lower-quality setups because "I need a win"
Feeling unlucky or cursed by the market

Protection Mechanisms:

 After 2 consecutive losses: Reduce position size to $75 margin (50% less)
 After 3 consecutive losses: Mandatory 24-hour break from trading
 Recognize each trade is independent (coin flip doesn't remember previous flips)
 Maintain position sizing discipline regardless of recent results
 Never increase risk to "recover" losses
 Track: "My strategy has X% win rate, variance is normal"

Recovery Protocol:

After losing streak, paper trade 3 winning setups before resuming
Return at half position size for next 5 trades
Only return to normal size after demonstrating discipline

2. Anchoring Bias Protection:
Definition: The tendency to rely too heavily on the first piece of information (the "anchor") when making decisions.
Manifestations:

Fixating on entry price ("I'm only profitable if it gets back to my entry")
Holding losers hoping to "break even" instead of cutting loss
Refusing to re-enter after stop-out because "I was just in at a better price"
Using past price levels as only reference (ignoring current structure)

Protection Mechanisms:

 Don't fixate on entry price - market doesn't care where you bought
 Re-evaluate market conditions with fresh perspective after each trade
 Adjust stops based on CURRENT structure, not past decisions
 If stopped out and setup reappears, treat as NEW trade (new entry, new stop)
 Don't hold losing position hoping to "get back to breakeven"
 Price is irrelevant, only structure matters

Mantra: "The market doesn't know or care where I entered. I make decisions based on NOW."
3. Recency Bias Protection:
Definition: Giving too much weight to recent information while ignoring broader context.
Manifestations:

"Market just went up 5%, it's going to keep going!" (ignoring resistance)
Abandoning strategy after one bad day
Overweighting last trade result in next decision
Ignoring daily/weekly trend because 5-min looks different

Protection Mechanisms:

 Don't overweight today's price action versus broader context
 ALWAYS check higher timeframes before reacting to lower timeframe
 Review weekly chart for perspective before every trade
 Ask: "Is this a new trend or just noise within existing trend?"
 Don't change strategy based on last 1-3 trades (need 50+ trade sample)
 Maintain statistical perspective: variance is normal

Checklist Before Every Trade:

What's the daily trend? (Overrides 15-min)
What's the weekly trend? (Overrides daily)
Is recent move part of bigger structure or anomaly?

4. Illusion of Control Protection:
Definition: The belief that one can control or influence outcomes that are actually random.
Manifestations:

Adding more indicators to "control" uncertainty
Over-analyzing to feel more "prepared"
Believing complex system = better results
Thinking "If I just study more, I'll predict every move"

Protection Mechanisms:

 Limit indicators to 3-4 maximum (MA, RSI, Volume, one optional)
 Focus on price action and volume PRIMARILY
 Accept that uncertainty cannot be eliminated
 Simple is better than complex (Occam's Razor)
 Acknowledge randomness: "I can't predict, I can only position for probable outcomes"
 If considering adding indicator, ask: "What decision will this change?"

Core Truth: Markets have inherent randomness. No amount of analysis eliminates it. The goal is managing probability, not achieving certainty.
Mantra: "I don't control the market. I control my risk, my position size, and my discipline."
5. Overconfidence Protection:
Definition: Believing one's knowledge or ability is more accurate than it really is, leading to underestimation of risk.
Manifestations:

"This setup is PERFECT, I'll risk more"
"I'm on a hot streak, time to increase size"
Ignoring risk management because "I've got this"
Underestimating how wrong you can be

Protection Mechanisms:

 Maintain trade journal reviewing ALL trades (not just winners)
 Track actual win rate and RRR results (know your real statistics)
 Never increase risk based on recent wins (position size stays consistent)
 Regularly review losing trades for lessons
 Remind yourself: "The market is supreme, I am a participant"
 After 3 consecutive wins: Take break, review, ensure not getting cocky

Statistics to Track:

Actual win rate vs expected
Actual average RRR vs planned
Largest winning streak and largest losing streak
Average win size vs average loss size
Expectancy: (Win Rate × Avg Win) - (Loss Rate × Avg Loss)

Humility Reminder: "Every expert was once a beginner. Every successful trader still experiences losses. I am never above the market."
